DevOS Islamic - sources of the Quran and Hadith texts
=====================================================

DevOS does not ship any Quran or Hadith text in the ISO and never generates
or edits one. When you turn DevOS Islamic on, it downloads these files once,
pinned to an exact git commit and checked against SHA-256 sums recorded in
/usr/local/share/devos/components/islamic/content.py. Every card shows the
reference (Surah:Ayah, or collection + hadith number) and the translator.

Stored in /var/lib/DevOS/islamic (shared by every account; written as root
through pkexec) or, if administrator approval is declined, in
~/.local/share/DevOS/islamic for that account only.

Quran - fawazahmed0/quran-api
  https://github.com/fawazahmed0/quran-api
  commit 47ca096b0976443ba2eab2e45cdf0fb4096a2610
  served from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/quran-api@<commit>/
  Dataset licence: The Unlicense (public domain dedication).
  Editions used (6236 ayahs each, 114 surahs):
    ara-quranuthmanihaf  Uthmani script, Hafs - King Fahd Glorious Quran
                         Printing Complex (qurancomplex.gov.sa), version 13
    ben-muhiuddinkhan    Bangla - Muhiuddin Khan (via tanzil.net)
    eng-ummmuhammad      English - Saheeh International (Umm Muhammad)
                         (via tanzil.net)
    info.json            surah names
  Upstream terms: the King Fahd Complex text may be used and shared but must
  not be altered. Tanzil states its translations are "for non-commercial
  purposes only"; other use needs the translator's or publisher's permission.
  DevOS is free software and does not sell these texts; anyone redistributing
  DevOS commercially with the texts pre-installed must check these terms.

Hadith - fawazahmed0/hadith-api
  https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api
  commit df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784
  served from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@<commit>/
  Dataset licence: The Unlicense (public domain dedication).
  Editions used:
    Sahih al-Bukhari  ara-bukhari (Arabic), ben-bukhari (Bangla, translator
                      not named by the dataset), eng-bukhari (Muhammad Muhsin
                      Khan) - 7589 entries
    Sahih Muslim      ara-muslim (Arabic), ben-muslim (Bangla, translator not
                      named by the dataset), eng-muslim (Abdul Hamid
                      Siddiqui) - 7563 entries
  DevOS shows only hadiths present in all three languages with a whole
  number (7526 from al-Bukhari, 7324 from Muslim). The translations belong
  to their translators and publishers; they are shown unchanged and credited.

Optional AI reflection
  The "Explain with DevOS AI" button asks the local model (on this PC) for a
  short reflection. It is labelled "AI reflection - may contain mistakes; not
  a religious ruling", is told never to quote or invent verses or hadith, and
  never replaces the texts above. Ask a qualified scholar for rulings.

Prayer times, Qibla and Hijri date are calculated on this PC (no network):
  methods Karachi (default), MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Umm al-Qura; Asr Hanafi
  (default) or Shafi'i; tabular Hijri calendar with a +/-2 day adjustment.
No adhan recording is included; you can pick your own sound file.
