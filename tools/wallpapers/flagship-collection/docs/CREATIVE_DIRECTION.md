# DevOS Flagship Wallpaper — Creative Direction

## Brand north star

DevOS is a **KDE Plasma–based custom desktop OS**. Wallpapers must feel like a confident, original OS identity — cyan accent `#38bdf8` on graphite/ink — not a Windows Bloom clone, not macOS Sonoma scenic IP, not purple SaaS gradients, not cream/terracotta lifestyle clichés.

## Signature look

| Axis | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Palette | Graphite `#0b1220`–`#1e293b`, ink navy, ice silver, cyan `#38bdf8`, restrained sapphire. Warm accents only as rare dual-tone luxury (champagne meeting cyan). |
| Materials | Frosted glass, satin ribbons, caustics, brushed metal, soft volumetric light, subtle film grain |
| Composition | Subject weighted **right / lower-right**; **calm left / upper-left** for Plasma icons & panels |
| Mood | Quiet monumentality — premium product photography energy, not neon club / crypto |

## Redesign principles (subject-aware)

1. **Never one LUT/preset** — each piece keeps its named subject (orbit ≠ bloom ≠ harbor).
2. **Native detail honesty** — do not claim 4K from upscaled 720p. New masters must be generated or truly higher-res sources.
3. **Theme fitness** — dark pieces stay graphite/cyan; light pieces stay pearl/ice with cyan accents; optional paired variants only when beneficial.
4. **Preserve originals** — redesigned files live under `flagship-collection/redesigned/`; originals untouched; `mapping.json` is reversible.

## Anti-patterns

- Purple haze / magenta SaaS glows as primary identity
- Busy left third (icons become illegible)
- Readable UI, logos, watermarks, fake terminal text
- Stretching non-16:9 masters into 16:9
- Palette clones (Ember/Rose/Tide) as “new” redesigns without unique subjects
